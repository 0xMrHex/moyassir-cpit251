# Target Audience Questionnaire (Google Form Instrument)

> **Instructions for Team:** Create a Google Form using the 6 questions below, distribute it to family, friends, and community members who have previously performed Hajj or Umrah with a campaign (aim for 15–30 responses), export Google Forms' auto-generated charts as PNG images into `docs/evidence/charts/`, and embed them in Chapter 1 of your Final Project Report.

---

## Survey Title
**Hajj & Umrah Campaign Logistics Experience Survey (CPIT251 Research)**

## Introduction Text
*We are Software Engineering students at King Abdulaziz University (FCIT) developing a web-based logistics portal for Hajj and Umrah campaigns. This anonymous 1-minute survey helps us understand real logistical challenges faced by attendees.*

---

## 6 Survey Questions

1. **Have you previously performed Hajj or Umrah through an organized campaign?** *(Multiple Choice — Required)*
   * Yes, Hajj
   * Yes, Umrah
   * Yes, both Hajj and Umrah
   * No (End of survey / general opinion)

2. **How did your campaign communicate your bus number, tent/room location, and daily itinerary?** *(Checkboxes — Select all that apply)*
   * WhatsApp groups / messages
   * Printed paper cards or lists
   * Verbal announcements by staff
   * Dedicated mobile app or web portal
   * SMS

3. **Did you or someone in your group experience confusion locating your assigned bus or tent/accommodation?** *(Multiple Choice)*
   * Frequently
   * Sometimes
   * Rarely
   * Never

4. **How quickly did you receive updates when departure times or schedules changed?** *(Linear Scale 1–5)*
   * `1` = Very slow / missed updates → `5` = Instant and clear

5. **How useful would a simple web portal be where you can log in with your National ID to check your exact bus number, driver phone, tent zone, and live schedule?** *(Multiple Choice)*
   * Extremely useful
   * Very useful
   * Moderately useful
   * Not useful

6. **What is the #1 logistical improvement you wish your campaign had offered?** *(Short Answer — Optional)*
